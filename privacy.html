Privacy Policy

This policy explains the types of information BeatTag may process when you use the platform.

Last updated: 13 September 2026

Information you provide

This may include account information such as name, username, email address, profile details, challenges, comments, tags, reports and media you choose to upload.

Authentication and cloud storage

BeatTag uses Supabase services for features such as authentication, database records, realtime updates and media storage. Information needed for those services may be processed by Supabase according to its applicable terms and privacy practices.

Device and app data

The web app may use browser storage, cached PWA assets and standard technical information needed to operate, secure and troubleshoot the service.

Camera, microphone and files

BeatTag only requests browser permissions when a feature needs them. Access depends on your device/browser permission settings. Uploaded media is processed only after you choose to capture or select it.

How information is used

Information may be used to provide accounts and profiles, display and share challenges, operate reactions/comments/chains, moderate reports, maintain security and improve platform reliability.

Public content

Challenges, media, usernames, comments and reactions posted to public areas may be visible to other users and may be shared by them. Avoid posting information you do not want to make public.

Data retention and deletion

Data may remain while an account or challenge is active, and for a reasonable period where needed for security, backups, moderation or legal obligations. BeatTag provides deletion controls for supported user content; associated storage cleanup may also occur.

Your choices

You can control browser permissions, avoid uploading optional media, delete supported content, log out and contact BeatTag regarding privacy questions.

Security

Reasonable safeguards are used, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security. Keep your password and verification codes private.

Policy changes

This policy may be updated as BeatTag evolves. The latest version will be posted on this page with a revised date.
