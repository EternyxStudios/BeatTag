About BeatTag

BeatTag is a mobile-first social challenge platform built around one simple idea: Beat it. Tag them. Keep it going.

Last updated: 13 September 2026

What BeatTag does

Create text, photo, video or audio challenges, post your own attempt, tag friends and grow a challenge from one generation to the next.

Challenge chains

Every “Beat This” attempt can continue the original challenge as a child challenge, creating visible Gen 1 → Gen 2 → Gen 3 and longer chains.

Community first

BeatTag includes likes, dislikes, comments, reports and moderation tools so people can participate, react and help keep the platform respectful.

Who builds BeatTag

BeatTag is an EternyxStudios project. The platform is under active development and features may improve over time.
