Community Guidelines

BeatTag is for fun, creative and competitive challenges—not harm, harassment or abuse.

Last updated: 13 September 2026

1. Keep challenges safe

Do not post or encourage challenges that could cause serious injury, self-harm, dangerous stunts, illegal acts or harm to other people, animals or property.

2. Respect other people

No bullying, targeted harassment, threats, hate, sexual exploitation, doxxing, impersonation or non-consensual intimate content.

3. Post content you have the right to share

Do not upload copyrighted, private or confidential material unless you have permission or another lawful right to use it.

4. Protect minors

Never sexualize minors, solicit exploitative content, or encourage dangerous activities involving children.

5. No spam or manipulation

Avoid repetitive spam, fake engagement, scams, phishing, malware, deceptive giveaways or attempts to manipulate platform systems.

6. Reporting and moderation

Use the report feature for content that may violate these guidelines. BeatTag may remove content, restrict features or accounts, and preserve information when reasonably necessary for safety or legal compliance.

If a situation involves immediate danger, contact local emergency services rather than relying only on an in-app report.
